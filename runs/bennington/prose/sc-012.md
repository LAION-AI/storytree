INT. AL'S DEPOT DINER - NIGHT

HALE stands. He comes around the counter.

CORA keeps her eyes on the register. The cassette sits hard in her apron.

                    HALE
              Look at me.

Her eyes come up. He holds them. The same still focus that just came off the tape.

                    CORA
              That's the look.

                    HALE
              Yes.

                    CORA
              You do that and I go down.

                    HALE
              You go down and tonight goes with you.

                    CORA
              Tape. Pictures. Everything I kept.

                    HALE
              Everything.

                    CORA
              Then you walk out with the last piece and I wake up empty.

                    HALE
              Yes.

His right hand lifts between them. Two fingers. Her face slackens.

                    CORA
              Don't.

The hand stops. Drops onto the sugar caddy. The fingers rest white on the lid and stay.

Her face comes back. Shoulders first. She does not step back. She does not turn a wrist up.

                    CORA
              You stopped.

                    HALE
              Yes.

                    CORA
              Midway.

                    HALE
              Yes.

                    CORA
              Why.

                    HALE
              You'd be no use after.

                    CORA
              That's the till talking.

                    HALE
              That's what I have.

                    CORA
              Your hand says different.

                    HALE
              My hand stopped.

                    CORA
              I can see it. I still need why.

                    HALE
              It would cost more than it got.

                    CORA
              Since when do you count that.

He looks at the fingers. They have not moved.

                    CORA
              You started it. That's on me now.

                    HALE
              I didn't finish.

                    CORA
              You started a check you won't ring. I felt it go in.

                    HALE
              It isn't in.

                    CORA
              You don't get to say what's in my head.

                    CORA
              You came around the counter for it.

                    HALE
              I did.

                    CORA
              And then you didn't.

                    HALE
              And then I didn't.

                    CORA
              You still need the last piece.

                    HALE
              Yes.

                    CORA
              And you won't take it like that.

                    HALE
              Not from you.

                    CORA
              Not tonight.

                    HALE
              Not from you.

                    CORA
              So from somebody else.

He does not answer.

                    CORA
              You didn't take anything. Not the night. Not blood.

                    HALE
              No.

                    CORA
              Money's still under the saucer.

                    HALE
              Keep it.

                    CORA
              Then get your hand off my sugar.

He takes the hand away. Steps back. Goes to the last booth. Leaves the bills where they sit.

At the kitchen door he holds the jamb. A tremor runs through that hand. His color has gone wrong.

                    CORA
              You're shaking.

                    HALE
              I know.

                    CORA
              How do you get it, then.

He does not answer. He goes out the back. The door settles.

She stays on her side of the counter. The sugar lid sits off true.
