EXT. FOUNDERS' STONE - NIGHT

Wet granite. Cut grass. A steeple clock that does not need to be right.

HALE comes up the last dark path. Polaroid in one hand. He does not rush the gate.

He holds the print to the face of the stone. Stained window. Moss. The same hour.

                        HALE
              Same window.

                        HALE
              Same moss.

                        HALE
              Same hour.

He kneels. Peels the moss from a cut at the base. The groove and the print agree.

                        HALE
              You took this.

                        HALE
              You don't have the night.

                        HALE
              It's still here.

He works two stamped pieces out of the cut. Takes two more from the coat. Four in one palm. The Polaroid stays in the other. He does not lay them down.

                        HALE
              One.

                        HALE
              Two.

                        HALE
              Three.

                        HALE
              Four.

                        HALE
              That's the set.

He starts to set them on the granite. Four points. The start of an order.

His hand stops.

                        HALE
              Stone first.

                        HALE
              Then the grass.

                        HALE
              Then the wall.

                        HALE
              First light I say it.

                        HALE
              Town's done.

He looks from the tokens to the print.

                        HALE
              You don't get a morning.

                        HALE
              The stack neither.

                        HALE
              This neither.

He turns the Polaroid. A time in a woman's hand on the white border. He holds the print against the four pieces.

                        HALE
              This.

The print.

                        HALE
              That.

The tokens.

                        HALE
              Not both.

                        HALE
              Cora.

Nothing answers. The steeple clock ticks a wrong minute.

He stands. It takes a count. One hand stays on the stone. The knees delay.

                        HALE
              Thank you.

He looks at the clock. At the shaded face of the stone. Dawn is not in the grass.

He opens his mouth on a formal sound. Looks at the print. Closes it.

He puts the four tokens inside the jacket. No line. No order. A pocket.

                        HALE
              Not yet.

The Polaroid he keeps out. He corrects his feet.

                        HALE
              Hours yet.

He stays by the stone. He does not speak the rest.
