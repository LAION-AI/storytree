INT. THE LODGE - DUSK

A fraternal back office. Radiator knocking. Paper, ink, a stamp pad, a lockbox. Last gray light in a high window.

Down the slope, the Hollow beer-sign goes dark.

ANTON VOSS is already at the desk. Cuffs even. Stamp-ink on the right index. The ledger open. A stamped vial in the lamp's circle.

He writes NIGHT 3 at the top of the page.

The landline rings. He takes it on the second ring.

                        VOSS
              Lodge.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              Hollow's dark. Set's done.

                        VOSS
              The new one.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              Still on the stool. Hasn't booked.

                        VOSS
              Night three and he sits. That's a hole.

He lifts the vial to the bulb. The stamp is sharp. The blood does not meet the etched line.

He turns it once. He sets it back in the tray with the others.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              You want me in.

                        VOSS
              I want the hours paid. Stay on the
              street.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              Then give me the night.

                        VOSS
              Four is closed.

He sets a ruler. He strikes through the 4 hard enough to cut the page. He writes 7 in the cut box.

                        VOSS (CONT'D)
              House is tightening. Unpaid hours make
              sloppy nights. Rate is seven.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              Since when.

                        VOSS
              Since now. Seven.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              That's a climb. They'll stiff. You
              put me on seven they run. They feed
              off the page. I spend the night
              chasing instead of collecting.

                        VOSS
              They feed off the page, the book is a
              hole with a cover. Seven brings them
              in or it names them. Both of those I
              can stamp.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              You can stamp a name. You can't stamp
              a man who already ate.

                        VOSS
              Then you walk him before he eats.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              Last time I walked four a man still
              swung.

                        VOSS
              Then four was the wrong number. This
              is the number that holds.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              Holds what.

                        VOSS
              A going concern. Tell them it's
              protection. Tell them seven buys a
              clean night.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              They won't hear protection.

                        VOSS
              They'll hear seven. That's enough.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              Give me an hour. I come in, we look
              at the stock, we pick a number that
              walks.

                        VOSS
              The number is seven. The stock is the
              stock. You don't come in to count it.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              I'm not in yet. Wait till I get there
              and we put it in the book together.

He writes PIKE in the enforcement column. The letters are neater than the 7. He underlines the name once.

                        VOSS
              For the minutes. Night three. House
              tightens on unpaid hours. Rate seven.
              Enforcement, Pike. You're in the book.
              That's the walk.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              I didn't come in.

                        VOSS
              I wrote you. Whoever is still on four.
              That's first.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              The unbound.

                        VOSS
              If he feeds off the page. If he pays
              seven he bought the night. That's the
              favour.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              And if he doesn't.

                        VOSS
              Then the hole gets closed. Same walk.

                        PIKE (O.S.)
              You coming down.

His eye goes to the short vial. He leaves it in the tray. He does not flatten the cut.

                        VOSS
              The desk is the desk. You walk it.

He hangs up. The ledger stays open. He stamps nothing.
