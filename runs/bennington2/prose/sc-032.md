EXT. CEMETERY PATH - DAWN

MAREN ELLISON walks the gravel. Long sleeve to the wrist. A canvas bag on her shoulder, not hidden. She does not hurry.

Frost on the headstones. First light on the chapel roof.

                        VOSS (O.S.)
          The window does not wait on a man in a door.

                        COLE (O.S.)
          Eight feet. I asked for the count.

The bag knocks her ribs. She leaves it there. She misses a step and takes the next one at the same speed.

INT. STONE CHAPEL - DAWN

The west door stands keyed open. A band of new light lies across the threshold stones.

COLE HARLAN holds the shade in the jamb. ANTON VOSS stands at the altar rail, finger on an open ledger. LENA CHO stands the tower side of the same rail, hands empty.

Lena is already looking at the light. Maren steps into it and stops beside Cole. She puts a hand on the jamb. She does not greet him. She does not ask.

                        MAREN
          Door's open.

                        COLE
          How many minutes from the shop.

She looks at the bag, not at him.

                        MAREN
          I walked.

                        VOSS
          Booked to the minute. That's a delivery on a line I hold.

                        COLE
          How long has the name been on the page.

                        VOSS
          Long enough it does not need her feet.

                        LENA
          Feet.

Maren does not look at them. She sets the bag on the threshold stone, in the light. She takes out a cassette. Sets that down beside it. Takes her hands away.

                        MAREN
          Tape. Deacon's book. The set times.

                        VOSS
          Ward writes. Writing isn't a stamp. I don't buy souvenirs off stock.

                        LENA
          Who is the tape for.

Cole's mouth opens.

                        MAREN
          The hours.

His mouth shuts. He looks at the cassette. He does not step into the light for it.

                        COLE
          How many hours.

                        MAREN
          I didn't sleep.

                        VOSS
          Then she's spoiling. I price her now or she walks and I still have the name.

                        COLE
          Read the number on that line.

                        VOSS
          You get a number when you declare. You get nothing from the door.

                        LENA
          Cole.

He does not answer her. The long sleeve has ridden up. Bruises sit on the inside of the wrist.

Voss looks at the wrist.

                        VOSS
          Used. I already wrote used.

                        LENA
          And you still brought it.

                        MAREN
          The shop keeps copies. I keep copies.

                        LENA
          Copies.

Maren keeps her eyes on the nave. She does not look at the ledger. She does not look at Lena.

                        COLE
          Stay in the shade.

                        MAREN
          I'm in it.

She does not blink long enough to lose the aisle. She stands.
