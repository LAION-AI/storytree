INT. MEMORIAL TOWER - DAWN

Stone stairs. A slit window. First light laid across the landing in a hard white bar.

LENA CHO comes up without a paper in her hand. Coat shut. She does not open it.

A maintenance door. She tries it. It opens.

INT. CHAPEL - CONTINUOUS

She steps in behind the pews.

COLE HARLAN holds the west doorway, notebook open. ANTON VOSS has the case on a bench, lid up, one finger on a line.

Her eyes touch Cole and leave him. She does not look at Voss. She does not look at the book.

                    COLE
          You started on my minute.

She is already moving. Glass ticks once in the coat. She leaves it there.

                    VOSS
          Stop. That door is not on a
          page.

                    COLE
          What landing. What count
          from the slit.

                    LENA
          Count.

                    COLE
          Look at me.

She does not. She puts both hands on the rail. Empty. First light is on the nave floor, a strip widening toward the pews.

                    VOSS
          Cho. You are in arrears. I
          priced you dull so nobody
          hunted you. Dull does not
          stand at my rail.

                    COLE
          You took last night.
          Four-fifty-one. Service
          door. Slit on the landing.
          You didn't pull a copy
          because you already walked
          it. You are one night. That
          is not a position.

                    LENA
          Walked.

                    VOSS
          Don't answer him.

                    COLE
          She isn't answering you
          either.

                    VOSS
          Favour. Hear it. I have
          vials that still hold. I
          have names that still feed.
          I have a page that has
          cleared this town. You have
          a coat and a closet. Sell
          me the coat. I stamp you, I
          tax you fair, I walk you
          out a door that is mine.

She keeps her hands on the wood. The strip of light takes another flagstone.

                    VOSS
          That is the only hour in
          this building that does not
          burn you for free. Get off.
          I don't lose two.

                    COLE
          Light is on the third
          stone. Eight feet of floor
          left that isn't lit.

                    VOSS
          I will not reopen a closed
          line for a guest who paid
          me just enough to be
          boring. I price the rail
          mine and I start with you.

                    COLE
          How many steps from this
          door to that rail.

                    LENA
          Steps.

                    COLE
          If you still—

                    LENA
          You're still in the door.

                    COLE
          Give me the number.

                    LENA
          Number.

                    VOSS
          Step off.

                    COLE
          Lena.

Her eyes start toward him. They stop short. They go to the light.

She does not open the coat. She does not give a name. She stands at the rail with her hands empty, and she waits.
