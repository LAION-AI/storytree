EXT. STONE CHAPEL - DAWN

First grey hits the cemetery wall. COLE HARLAN walks the last blocks. Vestry key already in his hand. He does not run.

                    COLE
          Eight.
          Eight even.
          Then the wall.

He turns the watch face-out. Reads it. Turns it face-in.

                    COLE
          Grey.
          False dawn.
          Four on the band.

Two other sets of feet, off the paths. One from the road. One from the green. Too far to name. He does not turn his head.

                    COLE
          Lodge road.
          Voss.
          Two if he ran.
          He won't run. He prices it first.

A thinner step on the green side.

                    COLE
          Green.
          Lena.
          What's her number.

Nothing answers him.

                    COLE
          She doesn't give one.

He reaches the west door. Puts the vestry key in the lock.

                    COLE
          Ordinary.

He turns it. The lock gives. He does not push the door wide. It stands open on the first light. A narrow band lies across the lip.

                    COLE
          It gives.
          Band's narrow.
          Shade holds.

He steps into the threshold. Stops in the jamb-shade. Does not cross the nave. One hand stays in the pocket.

Empty pews. Bare altar. No other body.

                    COLE
          Rail's empty.
          First.

The band has not finished the lip. He watches the stone. He does not look back down the path.

                    COLE
          Not yet.
          What's the number.

He opens his mouth. Closes it. The nave stays quiet.

                    COLE
          Seven.
          On me.

The other feet are still coming. Still too far.

                    COLE
          Lodge still two.
          Green still hers if she wants it.
          I don't look.

He looks anyway — only at the lip. The band finishes and holds. First light, full on the threshold. The shade still covers him if he does not step.

                    COLE
          Now.

He waits one measured breath. Even. No restart.

                    COLE
          One.

He keeps to the doorway shade. The key stays in the lock. He does not go to the rail.

                    COLE
          Window's open.
          I'm first.
          That's the count.
