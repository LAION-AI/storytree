INT. THE LODGE - DUSK

Back office. Radiator knocking. A blotter squared to the desk edge.

ANTON VOSS, stamp-ink on the right finger, cuffs kept clean. The hall clock strikes. He does not look up.

He opens a heavy ledger. Rules a fresh night column. Dates it.

The phone rings. He pins it with his shoulder.

                    VOSS
          Lodge.

                    VOICE (O.S.)
          You open?

                    VOSS
          I am now.

                    VOICE (O.S.)
          I need a name. Exclusive.

                    VOSS
          You need a line. I have three
          exclusives live. None of them
          are yours.

He stamps eight vials, one by one. Ink, then a look at the cap, then the next.

                    VOICE (O.S.)
          So open a fourth. I'll cover it.

                    VOSS
          You cover what I write.

He turns the book. Three names already in. He initials each, letters neater than his numbers. The pen stops over a blank line. He leaves it.

                    VOSS
          I'm not writing a fourth.

                    VOICE (O.S.)
          Then sell me an hour on one of
          yours. I'll make it good.

                    VOSS
          Shared hour on the second. You
          come to the door and I stamp it.
          Two in the tray thin by the third
          dawn. You want those, you buy the
          decay. I don't donate inventory.

                    VOICE (O.S.)
          That's a choke.

                    VOSS
          That's a floor. Door, or you watch
          the tray go pale without me.

                    VOICE (O.S.)
          How much for the hour.

                    VOSS
          I don't price a favour I can't
          put a mark on. Door.

He hangs up.

A knock. A RUNNER holds out a folded slip and waits. Voss finishes a stamp first. Then takes the slip.

                    RUNNER
          Green count. Forty.

Voss unfolds it. Does not smile. Writes a smaller figure in the night column.

                    VOSS
          Thirty-two.

                    RUNNER
          I said forty.

                    VOSS
          You said what you counted. I wrote
          what the house will carry.

He draws the difference into the house column. The runner watches the pen.

                    RUNNER
          That's my walk.

                    VOSS
          That's your walk if this page
          holds. Thirty-two. You're still
          on it.

                    RUNNER
          I brought you forty.

                    VOSS
          And I let you bring it. House
          takes eight. I don't run a column
          that learns zero.

                    RUNNER
          You want me back out there.

                    VOSS
          I want a count I don't have to
          correct. Tonight you got
          corrected. That's the cheaper
          stamp.

The runner stands a second too long. Then goes.

Voss sets the eight vials in the tray, soonest to fade in front. Leaves the ledger open. Looks at the lock. Leaves it.

He sits with the house column in front of him and watches the hall.
