INT. MOTOR COURT ROOM - NIGHT

The lot door opens. COLE misses the keyhole once, finds it, and stops in the threshold with the key still in the lock.

Ice machine buzzing through the wall. Curtains drawn. One bed. A desk.

He turns the watch on his wrist inward.

                        COLE
              Eleven minutes from the green.
              One down. Filed. Clearing.

He shuts the door. One step. He looks at the desk and does not take the second.

A spiral notebook sits on the blotter. The spiral does not meet the corner.

                        COLE
              No.

He crosses. Does not touch it. Finger on the blotter corner. Finger on the spiral. The gap is a half-inch.

                        COLE
              I squared you to the corner.
              You don't walk by yourself.
              Someone put you back.

He lifts the notebook off the desk. The blotter is empty where it sat. He holds the pages under the lamp.

                        COLE
              That's my hand.
              That's not my square.

He opens it. Columns. Times. Doors. A window.

                        COLE
              Two-fourteen. Door at the green.
              Two-twenty. Stage alley.
              Two-forty. Last mark I trust.

His finger stops under a blank run. The next line is his again. Later. It does not join.

                        COLE
              Interval.
              Give me the number.

He waits. The ice machine ticks. The book does not answer.

                        COLE
              Green to alley is six.
              Alley to last mark is twenty.
              Last mark to my next hand.
              It isn't on the page.

He flips forward. Back. The pen comes out of the same pocket. He checks the pocket first. Then the pen. Then the column.

A row of times with no doors. He keeps the pen on it.

                        COLE
              Access. Duration. Exit.
              That's a hunt.
              What's this row doing.
              No door. No walk. Just a clock.
              Against what.

He turns the watch inward. Does the gap against the last mark, flat, out loud.

                        COLE
              Two-forty.
              Then this.
              Four hours that aren't here.
              I can time a door.
              I can't make that return.

He looks at the door. At the key still in it. He stands.

                        COLE
              Tower is twelve.
              Twelve from here, twelve back.
              Twenty-four to confirm a night
              I already don't have.
              Twelve doesn't put it back.

He takes a step toward the door. Stops.

                        COLE
              You can copy a page.
              You can't square a desk.
              I have the half-inch.
              I don't need the stairs.

He comes back. Sits on the bed, not the chair. The book stays in his hands.

He closes it face-down. Hard. The cover bends under his palm. He does not flatten the bend.

He writes on the back cover. The number is in the room before the ink is.

                        COLE
              Three-ten.
              Late.

He underlines it once. Caps the pen. Same pocket.

The book stays face-down on his knee. His hands shake on the cover. He flattens them. They shake. He takes them off the page.

The shake stays. He swallows. The jaw works. The hand goes toward his mouth and drops.

He looks at the curtains. Not at the book.

                        COLE
              That's not the page.

Watch, face-in.

                        COLE
              Six.

He does not open the book.
