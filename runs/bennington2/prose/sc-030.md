EXT. STONE CHAPEL - DAWN

First light lies in a hard band on the west door. ANTON VOSS comes up the path from the Lodge. Ledger under one arm. A wooden case in the other hand. He does not hurry.

Cuffs even. Coat that used to be better. Stamp-ink on the right first finger.

The door is already keyed open. He takes that the way a clerk takes a desk that punched in without him.

INT. STONE CHAPEL - CONTINUOUS

Cold stone. Empty pews. A bare altar table. A rail.

COLE holds the doorway shade, just off the band. Watch worn face-in. A notebook in one hand. The key in the lock. He does not step.

Voss crosses him and sets the ledger on the rail. Sets the case beside it. Both hands come away.

The case sits there like a deposit on a counter.

He looks at Cole. Nods once. Creditor to a man who paid and then made a mess.

No price. No question.

                        VOSS
              Paid unbound. Filed.
              That was the courtesy.

Cole does not answer. Keeps the shade.

                        VOSS
              You still left a hole.
              I write holes neat.

He puts the stained finger on the case. He does not take a vial. He lifts the lid.

Twelve vials. Stamps dark, clean, current. The blood inside has gone thin. Light passes through it.

His hand hangs over the glass. He does not pick one up.

                        VOSS
              Twelve. Stamps hold.
              Contents light.

He leaves the lid up. Opens the ledger. Turns to a leaf he already knows. The ink-stained finger finds a line and stays there.

                        VOSS
              Ellison, M. Access.
              Standing charge. Holder:
              the book.

Silence from the door.

                        VOSS
              A set is a rate. A rate
              clears. Nobody bought her
              off the line. So the line
              stands.

He glances at the thin vials. Then at the name. He takes a pen. He writes ELLISON again, slower, neater than any number on the page.

                        VOSS
              You have a receipt for
              walking. You do not have
              a stamp on this name.

                        VOSS
              Lodge is shut. Collection
              is spent. The book is not.
              The hour belongs to a
              stamp. Not to a doorway.

Finger still on her line. He still does not touch a vial.

                        VOSS
              Unbound is closed. I do
              not reopen closed lines.
              This one is open.

He squares the case one centimeter. The blood in the glass stays thin. He does not look at Cole again.

                        VOSS
              Light or not. This page
              has cleared the town
              before. It will clear it.

He keeps the finger there. Ready. The lid stays up.
