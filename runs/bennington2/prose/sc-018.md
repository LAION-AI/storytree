INT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - HALL - NIGHT

    The last hour has emptied the rooms into the hall. A bottle goes over and stays down. Someone's coat is left on the stair.

    The peak noise breaks into smaller, meaner pieces. A record in another room skips and is left skipping.

    JULIAN holds the front door as if the house is still his to close. A WOMAN in last night's dress stands in the gap he makes.

                        JULIAN
              You were almost the room. You
              felt that.

                        WOMAN
              You locked it.

                        JULIAN
              The house locked it. I just
              didn't argue with the house.

                        WOMAN
              Then finish it. What was I.

    He smiles like the last word is still a gift. He does not put it down.

                        JULIAN
              Not in here. In here you're
              already leftover. Come out.
              I'll say it where it has some
              air.

                        WOMAN
              Say it here.

                        JULIAN
              If I say it here it sounds
              like a favor. I don't do
              those after the bottles go.

    He ushers her through. He is the last one treating the wreck as something he is offering.

    EXT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - PORTICO - NIGHT

    COLE is already in the portico shade. He watches the gravel, not the rooms. The watch is face-in. He does not look at it.

                        COLE
              Forty-one minutes.

    Julian does not look at him. He walks the woman out onto the stones and stops her there, one step past the shade line.

                        JULIAN
              He brought a clock to a
              party. It's almost a
              personality.

                        WOMAN
              I'm cold. Say it.

                        JULIAN
              You were the best thing in
              that hall that wasn't the
              house. You were very close to
              being—

    He stops. She waits for the noun. He withholds it the way he withheld the key.

                        COLE
              East side first. Drive after.
              Thirty-nine.

                        JULIAN
              Clocks are what people invent
              when the room stops choosing
              them.

                        WOMAN
              Julian.

                        JULIAN
              Almost invited. Almost kept.
              Almost worth opening again.
              You hear how all of those are
              generous.

                        WOMAN
              That's not a finish.

                        JULIAN
              It's the only finish that
              still has taste.

                        COLE
              Thirty-six. That's the
              margin.

                        JULIAN
              Don't number my drive.

    First grey is not on the stones yet. Cole stays in the shade and writes nothing.

                        WOMAN
              I'm going in.

                        JULIAN
              After the word. Stay for the
              word. People remember the
              word.

                        COLE
              They don't. Thirty-four.

    EXT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - DRIVE - DAWN

    First light takes the gravel in one pass. The drive is no longer shade.

    Down the hill the town goes the same grey. Night leaves the roofs together.

                        WOMAN
              That's morning.

                        JULIAN
              That's a rumor with good
              lighting.

    He laughs at the margin the way he laughed at the key. He does not come back in.

    The woman walks through the grey toward the door. The light does nothing to her. She does not look back.

                        COLE
              The drive is done.

    Julian looks at the strip of shade under the portico. Looks at Cole. Does not take it.

                        JULIAN
              Crawl under there with the
              little book? You almost had a
              night. You almost—

    He leaves the compliment unfinished. He laughs at the gap.

    Cole's mouth opens. He does not use the name. He does not tell him to come in.

                        COLE
              Seven.

    The number is already wrong. Light takes the rest of the stones.

    Julian's laugh breaks in the middle. He looks at his hands as if they failed a dress code. He goes to one knee, then the other.

    It is not long. It is not a show. What is left does not get up.

    Cole does not move into the light.

    INT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - UPSTAIRS WINDOW - DAWN

    MAREN stands in a long sleeve. The high has gone to grit in her face. She looks once at the drive. Then at her own hands.

    EXT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - PORTICO - DAWN

    Cole has the notebook open in the shade. He writes two words. He stops. He does not write the next line. He closes it on that.

    Maren is in the doorway behind him, still in the sleeve, still out of the light. She does not come down the step.

                        MAREN
              What's on the page.

                        COLE
              A rival. Removed.

                        MAREN
              That's the drive.

                        COLE
              That's a name less.

                        MAREN
              Read the rest.

                        COLE
              There isn't a rest.

    She looks at her hands again. The bruises sit where the sleeve can hide them. She names the sleeve instead.

                        MAREN
              This kept. The tape kept.
              That didn't.

                        COLE
              Morning.

                        MAREN
              Don't number that.

    Neither of them goes down to the gravel. The record inside is still skipping. No one lifts the needle.
