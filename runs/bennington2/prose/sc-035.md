INT. STONE CHAPEL - DAWN

VOSS has the ledger open on the altar rail. His hand on the vial case. A finger on a line.

                    VOSS
          Ellison. Standing credit. I
          declare it.

He stamps the margin. The page does not clear.

                    VOSS
          I booked that name night one.
          The line covers a stake. I
          declare it in my column.

                    COLE
          It's written in hers.

                    VOSS
          It's a drawer. Drawers move.
          Singer. Credit in your
          column. I collect it. You
          keep the tape. You keep the
          door. I take the number off
          the page.

                    MAREN
          That's my name.

                    VOSS
          I'm offering to clear it
          for you.

                    MAREN
          The tape's already mine.

                    VOSS
          Then sell me the line. Two
          nights' keep. Rent on the
          walk-up. The Thursday set
          stays yours. I pull every
          other name off the page.
          That is the price.

                    MAREN
          Nobody else is writing.

                    COLE
          Standing in her name. Not a
          claim. A credit.

                    VOSS
          I ran the book. The book
          holds the credit. I am the
          book.

                    COLE
          What's the number on the
          line.

                    VOSS
          Seven. Declared. Enough to
          open the window.

                    COLE
          Seven of what.

                    VOSS
          Of hers. Three nights. You
          initial. I stamp. We are
          done.

                    MAREN
          No.

                    VOSS
          Pike already collected.
          This is paper. Initial it.

                    COLE
          Pike's not in the room.

                    VOSS
          He does not have to be.

                    MAREN
          That's still my name.

                    VOSS
          I am not asking you to like
          the price.

                    MAREN
          I didn't say please.

He writes his name over hers in a neat hand. The ink sits wet on top. The column does not clear.

                    VOSS
          The line is mine.

                    COLE
          She didn't initial.

                    VOSS
          Twelve stamped. Tax paid.
          They read as blood.

                    COLE
          Open one.

He snaps a vial on the rail. The stamp on the glass is clean.

Thin. Brown. It runs between the stones and does not hold.

                    VOSS
          Stamp is good.

                    COLE
          The stock isn't.

He snaps a second. Same stamp. Same thin.

                    VOSS
          I moved these last night.

                    COLE
          Count the color.

                    VOSS
          Ten left. Same stamp. Same
          night.

                    COLE
          Same water.

He sets both necks down. He looks at the ten still sealed. He does not take a third.

                    VOSS
          The tax was paid. The book
          is solvent. Pike is
          downtown. Nine still
          feeding.

The light on the threshold widens. He is still at the rail.

                    VOSS
          Pike.

No answer from the street.

                    VOSS
          Nine.

                    VOSS
          Window's gone.

                    COLE
          It went.

He shuts the ledger. The wet ink smears his name, not hers. He leaves the case on the rail.

                    VOSS
          I ran it correctly. Clean
          book. Nothing to declare.

He steps off the rail. He does not look at COLE. He does not look at the singer. He takes the side aisle, out of the light, still in the room.
