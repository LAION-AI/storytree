EXT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - NIGHT

Cole comes up the long drive on foot. Watch worn face-in. He stops once, checks it, starts again. He does not run.

INT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - FRONT HALL - NIGHT

The party is already a gathering. Coats on the banister. A stereo working too hard through one bad speaker. In the doorway: a tray. Glasses. A shallow dish. A rolled bill.

Julian holds the door he can still close. Open collar. He smiles as if the room had already improved.

                        JULIAN
          Harlan. You walked the hill.

                        COLE
          Fourteen from the green. The
          drive added six.

                        JULIAN
          And you counted it before you
          took a glass. That's almost
          a way to arrive.

He puts a glass in Cole's hand. Tips the dish toward him.

                        JULIAN
          If you refuse the tray I have
          to hear the reason. I don't
          want a reason. I want you in
          the house.

                        COLE
          What's the count tonight.

                        JULIAN
          Enough that a number would be
          rude.

                        COLE
          Then the rooms.

                        JULIAN
          Some of them open. The better
          ones open if I like the ask.
          You don't ask. You measure
          the hinges.

Cole drinks. Uses the bill. He takes both the way he takes a reading. No pleasure in it.

                        JULIAN
          There. Now you're late in a
          more expensive way.

                        COLE
          It'll move the timing.

                        JULIAN
          Good. That's a house doing
          what a house is for.

Cole looks toward the stairs. A woman remains on them and does not come down.

                        COLE
          Cho.

                        JULIAN
          She already asked for a door.
          I told her what that costs.
          She's deciding if the
          question was pretty.

                        COLE
          How many doors did you keep.

                        JULIAN
          All the ones people still
          want.

He turns to the next coat. The compliment is already dying in his mouth.

Cole clears the front hall with the glass still in his hand. His line through the room is no longer straight. He sees that. He does not write it down.

INT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - LANDING - NIGHT

One sconce is dead. The other is yellow and unkind. Band cases stacked where they were dropped. No stage. No set list.

Maren stands in that light. Street clothes that already did a set. She tugs a sleeve down over a bruise it does not quite cover.

Cole stops in the threshold. He counts who crosses. He does not cross yet.

                        COLE
          They put you here.

                        MAREN
          He said the good room was for
          later.

                        COLE
          Later has a time.

                        MAREN
          Not one he put on paper.

A server comes through with the tray. Maren takes a glass. She takes the bill. Shop hands. She does not ask anyone for either.

                        MAREN
          If I stay empty I go down. If
          I go down I sleep.

                        COLE
          How many nights.

                        MAREN
          Two. I'm still on this one.

                        COLE
          The light's wrong for a set.

                        MAREN
          It isn't a set. It's parking.
          He brought the band and then
          he put the band where nobody
          has to look at us.

She drinks. She uses the bill. The hit lands in her shoulders first. She stays on her feet.

Cole steps out of the threshold. Closer than the space he usually keeps. He does not correct it.

                        COLE
          What's still holdable.

                        MAREN
          The glass. The sleeve. What's
          on the tape if the shop
          didn't sell it.

                        COLE
          I asked for a number.

                        MAREN
          I don't have one that stays
          past a bed.

                        COLE
          A bed ends the count.

                        MAREN
          That's the work. Not the
          song. The work is remaining
          upright.

                        COLE
          What time did he tell you
          you'd sing.

                        MAREN
          He didn't. He told the room
          there would be music. Then
          he put the cases here.

Julian is on the upper flight, smiling at the bad light as if he had invented it.

                        JULIAN
          There. I saved you something.
          You'll like the lock. It's
          the only honest piece of
          hardware in the house.

                        COLE
          Give me the hour the east
          windows take light.

                        JULIAN
          Early. Early enough that
          people who stay interesting
          stop asking. Come up.

INT. WHITLOCK HOUSE - UPSTAIRS HALL - NIGHT

He walks them. He does not offer an arm. The hall is already filling: bodies testing doors, a laugh trying too hard.

He stops them at a good door. Old lock. He turns it so they can hear it take.

                        JULIAN
          Listen. It turns like it
          chose you. I almost gave
          this one to Cho. Then I
          thought of the two of you
          standing in my bad light,
          and I thought...

                        COLE
          The key.

Julian holds it up. Brass. He lets them look at it. He puts it back on his own ring. He smiles as if the looking were what he had offered.

                        JULIAN
          A room you can walk into is
          just a room. This way you
          still want the house.

                        MAREN
          That's a key. It's still on
          you.

                        JULIAN
          And you said it without
          asking. That's... almost the
          reason I brought you.

                        COLE
          How long do we stand here.

                        JULIAN
          Until standing feels like
          you failed the lock. Then
          you can thank me for the
          hall. The hall never
          pretended it was given.

                        MAREN
          He parked us twice.

                        JULIAN
          I placed you. Placement is a
          compliment. You can decline
          it by leaving. You haven't.

Below them the stereo jumps. The gathering breaks into peak: a glass gone, someone already at the next door that will not open, more bodies coming up because they saw a key that did not change hands.

                        JULIAN
          See? Now it's a night.

He leaves them. The key catches the sconce on his way down. He does not look back.

Cole's hand goes to the watch. He turns the face out. The number is bad. He turns it back in. The other hand checks the pen in its pocket and leaves it there.

Maren faces the locked door. She starts on the object, not the hour.

                        MAREN
          The hour is still this
          hour. I had the rest of—

A YOUNG MAN comes out of the next room. Collar open. Color high in his neck. He looks at Cole like another door.

Cole does not look at Maren.

He takes the young man by the wrist and puts him against the wall. Close. Closer to her than it needs to be.

The young man starts a sound.

The hall takes the rest.

The young man sits where he is put. A smear on the collar. He is breathing. He is not looking at anyone.

Color is back in Cole that the walk up the hill had taken. He wipes his mouth with the back of his wrist. Checks the watch.

He still does not look at her.

                        MAREN
          You didn't look.

                        COLE
          No.

                        MAREN
          That one isn't me.

                        COLE
          No.

                        MAREN
          I had a sentence. The hour
          was still this hour.

                        COLE
          I know.

She waits. He does not give the sentence back. He also does not take the far wall. He stays where the feed put him. The space between them is just gone.

                        MAREN
          In the morning this hall
          goes. The collar stays. The
          sleeve stays. What you just
          did stays on him.

                        COLE
          Morning hasn't collected.

                        MAREN
          And if I sleep.

                        COLE
          Then you don't keep the hall.

                        MAREN
          Give me the number on that.

He gets the notebook halfway out of his coat. No line goes in. He puts it back.

                        COLE
          I don't have one.

                        MAREN
          You always have one.

                        COLE
          Not for this.

Below them a door slams. Julian laughs. Someone thanks him for a room they did not get.

Maren looks at the locked door. Then at Cole. She does not ask. She does not step away.

                        MAREN
          So we stay in the wreck.

                        COLE
          Until the clock says
          otherwise.

                        MAREN
          Yours or mine.

He doesn't answer. He also doesn't put the distance back.

The east windows at the end of the hall are still dark.
