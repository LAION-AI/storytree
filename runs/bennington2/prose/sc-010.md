EXT. CAMPUS NIGHT BEAT - NIGHT

A single sodium lamp over wet grass. A plywood checkpoint. A kettle. A clipboard chained to the stand.

MAREN ELLISON comes up the path in her stage coat. She does not call out.

DEACON WARD stands under the lamp with a sweat-warped pocket book shut against his chest. He does not look up until a scuffed cassette hits the clipboard.

She leaves it there like another form. A bruise sits on her wrist where the sleeve has ridden. She waits.

                    DEACON
          Eleven-forty. West lot.
          Broken bottle. No complainant.
          Glass left on the ground.

He does not ask how she is. He does not touch the tape. He opens last night's book and keeps his eyes on the page.

                    DEACON (CONT'D)
          Twelve-oh-five. Dormitory
          row, south. Window left open.
          Closed it. Nobody home.

                    MAREN
          Last night.

                    DEACON
          This is last night.

                    MAREN
          The tape.

                    DEACON
          It's on the board.

                    MAREN
          Type two. No card. Not stock.

                    DEACON
          I don't take tapes. I take
          times.

He turns a page with his thumb.

                    DEACON (CONT'D)
          Twelve-twenty. Library steps.
          Bicycle on its side. No lock.
          Left it.

                    MAREN
          Read or I take the book.

                    DEACON
          You don't take the book.

                    DEACON (CONT'D)
          Twelve-thirty-eight. Safety
          office. Kettle boiled dry.
          No one signed the log after
          ten.

She stays on her feet. The bruise does not get a line.

                    DEACON (CONT'D)
          Twelve-forty-seven. Alley
          behind The Hollow. Noise
          complaint. Caller wouldn't
          leave a name.

Her hand lands on the page.

                    MAREN
          That one.

                    DEACON
          Quiet when I got there.
          Nothing on the ground. Door
          locked. I wrote quiet.

She lifts the cassette off the clipboard and sets it on that line. Holds it there until the time and the plastic occupy the same inch.

                    MAREN
          The voice cuts.

                    DEACON
          I wrote quiet.

                    MAREN
          Twelve-forty-seven.

He looks at the cassette. He looks at the time. He does not add a word.

                    DEACON
          One-oh-five. Science annex.
          Door found unlocked. Relocked.
          No one inside.

                    MAREN
          I said that one.

                    DEACON
          One-oh-five comes after
          twelve-forty-seven.

                    MAREN
          Write the tape.

                    DEACON
          I didn't take the tape. I
          took the complaint.

                    MAREN
          The quiet is on both.

                    DEACON
          The quiet is what I found.
          I don't have a tape in the
          book.

She keeps her fingers flat on the Hollow line so the page cannot turn.

                    MAREN
          You're the one who writes
          it down.

                    DEACON
          I write what I took.

                    MAREN
          Then take it.

                    DEACON
          I already closed the hour.

He turns what she is not holding.

                    DEACON (CONT'D)
          One-twenty-two. Faculty lot.
          Car running, nobody in it.
          Engine off. Keys not present.

                    MAREN
          Don't turn it.

                    DEACON
          One-forty. North path. Coat
          on a bench. Still there at
          two. Left it.

The cassette stays on twelve-forty-seven. She does not ask again. She does not say please.

He looks at her hand. He looks at the bruise. He looks back at the book.

                    DEACON (CONT'D)
          I can copy the page.

He pulls a blank form from under the clipboard clip and writes without commentary. Time. Place. Noise complaint. No name. Quiet on arrival. Nothing left on the ground.

She takes the copy before the last period is dry. She does not thank him.

He writes in the book.

                    DEACON (CONT'D)
          Two-eighteen a.m. Ellison
          took a copy. Twelve-forty-
          seven Hollow complaint.
          Cassette on the board. Not
          logged as evidence. Not
          mine.

He turns the book so the new line faces her. He does not ask if she wants it read.

                    MAREN
          That's the object.

                    DEACON
          That's what you took.

He closes the cover. The book goes back in the jacket. The clipboard is empty except for the chain.

He picks up the kettle, finds it cold, sets it down, and starts down the path as if the exchange were a finished incident.

She stays under the lamp. Tape in one hand. The copy in the other.

She puts the written time against the cassette window and holds them there, thumb on both, like counting stock.

The unread pages leave with him.
