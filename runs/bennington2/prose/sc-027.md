INT. MAREN'S WALK-UP - DUSK

MAREN comes in off the stair and shuts the door. Bag under her arm. She does not go to the bed.

She hangs the bag on the chair. Stands. Hand toward the seat. She takes it back.

                        MAREN
              Chair later.
              Bed's a close.

Grey already on the landing window. Thin. November.

She empties the bag. Cassette. Pocket book. Folded set-list. She lays them left to right with the old notes.

                        MAREN
              Tape. Shop copy.
              Book. Deacon. Pencil still in it.
              Set times. My hand.
              Left to right. That's the drawer.

Lamp. Click. Click.

Sink. Sleeve to the elbow. The mirror takes the bruises. Two. Dark. Where a cuff lives.

                        MAREN
              Sleeve. Two.
              Not face.
              Not throat.
              Work.

She leaves them showing. Water on. Instant in a cup. She stays up.

Till slam downstairs. Shutter. Keys. The shop door locks. The street goes thin.

                        MAREN
              Shop's down.
              I'm not.
              Kettle's on. That's a shift.

She pours. Hits PLAY.

The deck turns. Glasses. A room.

                        MAREN (ON TAPE)
              That's ten-forty. One more then I'm off.
              Don't talk through it.

                        MAREN
              Ten-forty.
              Second song.
              Hollow mike. That's the room.

                        MAREN (ON TAPE)
              Leave the glass where it is.
              I'm not done with it.

Her hand goes flat on the table so the rest of her will not follow. She thumbs the pocket book open.

                        MAREN
              Nine-twelve. Lot check.
              Nine-forty. Hollow door.
              Nine fifty-eight. Pike. Paid.
              Ten-oh-six. Bar.
              Ten-forty. Set.
              Ten forty-two. Maren. Closed.

She does not slow down for her name. She turns the page.

                        MAREN
              Ten-fifty. Alley.
              Eleven. Lot.
              Eleven-twenty. Blank.
              Book jumps. That's a hole.

                        MAREN (ON TAPE)
              That's three if they tip.
              If they don't I still do four.
              Last call is a time. It is not a favour.

                        MAREN
              Eleven-ten if they tip.
              Book doesn't have eleven-ten.
              Fourth song. No line.
              I still sang it.

She sets the cassette on the open page.

                        MAREN
              You wrote closed.
              Closed. Says who.
              Tape says I was still on.
              Book says closed.
              Set says four.
              All three stay.
              None of you get to pick.

The voice on the tape thins. The last word is coming. She looks at the bed.

She sits on the edge of it. Two seconds. She stands up off it.

                        MAREN
              That's a close.
              Not mine.
              Not tonight.

She hits STOP. Her recorded voice dies mid-word. She does not let it go quiet. She jogs the deck one flick back. Leaves it.

                        MAREN
              Cued.
              Mid. Not the end.
              Tape. Book. Times.
              That's the lot.
              Drawer stays open.

She drinks. It burns. She keeps the cup.

She sings the line again. Flatter. Not louder.

                        MAREN
              Leave the glass where it is.
              I'm not done with it.

She buttons the long sleeve over the marks. One. The next. Cuff. She checks the mirror. Street clothes. Clean.

                        MAREN
              Cuff.
              Street.
              Street doesn't get stock.
              Table does.

She sits in the chair. Faces the table. The bed stays empty behind her.

Sash leak. Radiator tick. The grey dies in the window.

INT. MAREN'S WALK-UP - NIGHT

Lamp only. She does not hit PLAY. She does not lie down.

                        MAREN
              Two up.
              Chair is three.
              Lights stay.
              Copies stay.
              I stay.

She keeps her eyes open. She is still in the chair.
