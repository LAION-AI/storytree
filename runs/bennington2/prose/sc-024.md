INT. CAMPUS SAFETY OFFICE - NIGHT

A kettle clicks off. DEACON WARD at the desk, jacket on, warped pocket notebook open under the lamp. He writes a time. The phone rings. He finishes the line. Picks up.

                        DEACON
              Campus safety. Ward. Time and address.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              I don't want a report with my name
              on it. There's a collection on Depot.
              Side street. After hours. They're in
              the house and they're loud and I
              want it quiet, that's all.

                        DEACON
              Depot. The number.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              Forty-one. Not the shop. The house.
              I don't know the people.

                        DEACON
              Forty-one Depot. Time.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              After midnight. I looked at the clock.
              Twelve-ten they started. I'm not
              coming down there.

Deacon turns a page back. Finger stops. He reads what is already written.

                        DEACON
              Twelve-ten. North path. Footsteps.
              No name. No close. Copy to Ellison.
              Already out. Same clock. No address
              on that line. Give me the address
              again.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              Forty-one Depot. I said it. I didn't
              ask for a man at the door. I asked
              for quiet. It isn't even campus.

                        DEACON
              Names on the premises.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              I don't have names. They're not
              students. One of them said collection.
              A debt. After hours. That's the word
              they used. That's all I heard.

                        DEACON
              Anyone hurt on the premises.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              I didn't stay to see. I'm not going
              back over there.

                        DEACON
              Your name.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              No. Forget I called. It just went
              quiet. Don't write forty-one. Don't
              write me. Don't come.

                        DEACON
              Refused. I am writing refused.
              Forty-one Depot. Twelve-ten.
              Collection after hours. No student
              named. No caller named.

He writes the address. Lifts the pen. Starts the same address again, slower.

                        CALLER (O.S.)
              I take it back. It isn't yours.
              You don't have to come. Wait.
              Wait for a second call. Please.

                        DEACON
              Forty-one Depot. Twelve-ten.
              Collection. After hours. Second
              line. It is in the book.

He hangs up. Closes the notebook on the double line. Stands. Pocket book into the coat.

The desk phone. A card: ELLISON, a local number. His hand on the receiver. He does not lift it.

He takes the hand back. Kills the kettle. Opens the door.

                        DEACON
              Ellison not called. No second
              complaint. Ward off desk.

He goes.
