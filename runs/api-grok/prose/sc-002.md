EXT. HIGHKETTLE VILLAGE - NIGHT

Three people come in on the frozen track. A thin fire shows in the forge-house upslope. A bellows works, then a hammer.

They do not take the forge path. They go to the meeting shed.

INT. MEETING SHED - NIGHT

A chest stands against the wall. The FIRST RAIDER sets a bar into the lock. The SECOND RAIDER holds a lamp. The THIRD RAIDER keeps the door.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          That's their smithy.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          I hear it. Leave it. They're on a heat.
          They won't come off a heat for a lock.

                    THIRD RAIDER
          After this we go up?

                    FIRST RAIDER
          We don't go up. We don't search the
          houses. We're three. We walk out with
          what three can carry.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          We walked in with nothing.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          Then we don't get greedy. Hold the light.
          It's a village lock.

The lock breaks. He lifts the lid.

Cloth. A short stamped bar. Unfinished bars in a short pile.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          There. That's the piece.

                    THIRD RAIDER
          And the year's iron.

The first raider lifts the stamped bar and turns it. He does not weigh it.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          Hold on. It's light for that stamp.
          Give it here.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          No. We're not weighing it. We've got
          no true bar and we've got no time.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          I'm only saying the face and the mass
          don't sit.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          I felt it. Wrap it. We take it. One
          thing. The thing the valley will buy.

He wraps it.

                    THIRD RAIDER
          That's what they show at midwinter.
          They set it out with the iron.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          That's why it moves. One of us can
          walk with this. Not this and the stack.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          That's a winter of bars. We could eat
          off those if we got them down.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          Lift them then. All of them. And this.
          And the pass after. Go on.

The second raider lifts two bars and puts them back.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          I can't.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          None of us can. So the stack stays.
          We don't go to the forge for more of
          the same weight we already can't lift.

                    THIRD RAIDER
          Empty the chest and leave the iron
          sitting?

                    FIRST RAIDER
          The chest will be empty of what we
          came for. Their iron can sit. Shut it.

The second raider shuts the chest. The lock hangs broken.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          They'll see it empty at first light.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          Then they see it.

                    THIRD RAIDER
          Leave a mark? A word?

                    FIRST RAIDER
          We don't post a claim. We don't leave
          a message. We came because we're hungry
          and our slope gets none of their
          protection. That's the whole of it.
          Not their rite.

He puts the wrapped bar inside his coat.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          Out. Same way we came.

EXT. HIGHKETTLE VILLAGE - NIGHT

They come back onto the track. The first raider keeps the piece at his side. They do not look at the houses.

The forge fire is still up. The hammer keeps on. No door opens. No one comes down.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          They'll send after us. Midwinter isn't
          far.

                    THIRD RAIDER
          Without that weight they put nothing
          on the cloth.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          Hold wants the piece and the iron both.

                    THIRD RAIDER
          They've got no spare. No coin to buy
          one. No one to hire.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          No fighters either. Same as us. They
          can walk the pass and try to take it
          back or they can stand at the showing
          with a short pile and an empty hand.

                    SECOND RAIDER
          Empty hand, the Hold keeps the grain.
          Shuts the pass on them for a year.

                    FIRST RAIDER
          That's their compact. Not ours. Walk.

They take the track toward the winter pass. The shed is dark. The path stays empty.
