# PLOT Layer v8 — Assessment

**Overall Score: 5/10**

**Distinctness of Stances (6/10):** The five throughlines are conceptually separable but unevenly executed. *Awakening* (truth vs. lie) and *Betrayer* (meaning vs. pleasure) are sharply distinct — the former tracks Neo's irreversible choice, the latter Cypher's inverted mirror of that choice. *Reluctant One* (relationship/identity-faith) and *Hunter/Chosen* (society/freedom vs. determinism) both orbit the Oracle's prophecy and Smith as enforcer, creating substantive overlap; they are saved only by stance — internal withholding vs. external enforcement. *Rescue* (sacrifice vs. preservation) is thematically clear but shares its climax beats (ev-032, ev-035, ev-045) with Reluctant One, blurring exclusivity.

**Causal Chains (6/10):** *Betrayer* (ev-020→029) is tightest: bargain→beacon→trap→murder with explicit enablement. *Awakening* (ev-004→013) is linear-physical: contact→pursuit→capture→extraction→choice→birth. *Reluctant One* sustains 13 links of psychological causality (naming→failure→retreat→denial→rebirth). *Rescue* core (ev-025→032) is tight but bookended by filler. *Hunter* has a coherent 4-link spine (ev-014→023→039→045) drowned by filler gaps.

**Coverage Repair (3/10):** 18/≈50 links use boilerplate `(coverage repair: bridging link)` — zero causality (ev-047 plus 12/16 in Hunter). Where prose is attempted, rationales are strained: ev-005 "mundane morning as before," ev-017 intimacy as Rescue stake, ev-001–003 Sentinel cameos as Hunter enforcement. *Hunter* is 75% repair — a catch-all, not a stance. *Betrayer* (zero repairs) proves tight chains need no padding.

**Verdict:** Three plots show distinct, causally dense stances achievable by the layer; *Hunter* and *Rescue* reveal coverage pressure overriding integrity — repairs are explicitly labeled rather than blended, defeating the design goal.
