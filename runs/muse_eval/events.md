# EVENT Layer Build 10 — Assessment (ev-001–ev-008)

**Overall: 6.5/10** — Causal summaries strong; participant/location hygiene and state-change concreteness uneven.

**Causal Summary (4/5):** Summaries are the layer's strength — each condenses 1–7 scenes into a single reversible turn with clear `turns_on` and `boundary_reason` (ev-001 cuff-as-trigger, ev-003 hardline→payphone, ev-006 ledge-faith failure, ev-007 mouth-seal). Actions distinguish `what happened` from `so what` (ev-002 split, ev-005 reassertion). Only ev-003 overloads (7 scenes, 17 entities) into a breathless list; still causal, but at compression limit.

**Participants / Locations (2/5):** Locations plausible but buggy: ev-001 repeats `HEART O' THE CITY HOTEL` ×3, ev-004 splits `NEO'S APARTMENT/APARTMENT`, ev-006 uses `SKYSCRAPER` not present elsewhere. Participants conflate agents with props: ev-001 lists `a flashlight, guns, modem, fold-up table` (10 objects among 17), ev-004 `industrial music, shifting wall of bodies`, ev-005 `clock-face, ALARM CLOCK` as persons; ev-006 `_roster` has 13 but `participants` 11 (drops `FEDERAL EXPRESS GUY, a cop`). `THE PRESENCE` entities are treated as participants, inflating counts and masking real cast.

**State Changes (3/5):** When populated, triples are concrete and evidence-linked (ev-001 BIG COP dead, Neo mouth-sealed ev-007, Neo handcuffed ev-006). But 4/8 events have empty `{}` registers (ev-006 Smith/Trinity/Morpheus/props, ev-005 RHINEHEART), ev-002 tracks only positional, and vague verbs (`transitioned`, `replaced`, `suppressed`) replace entry→change→exit rigor. Knowledge/safety often restates `unchanged_because` tautologically.
